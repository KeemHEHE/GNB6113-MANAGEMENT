# GNB6113/GNBF6113 Management Assignment (30%) — Task Outline
**Title:** Reflective Analysis on Managing Change and Human Resource Practices in Contemporary Organizations
**Deadline:** Week 6 — 25 September 2026 (no late submission)
**Format:** Written report (≤16 pages, TNR 12pt, 1.15 spacing, 1" margins) + 5–7 min video presentation

---

## Step 1 — Setup (before writing starts)
- [ ] Form group of 5–6 (same tutorial session)
- [ ] Pick group name
- [ ] Appoint team leader (responsible for final Ebwise submission)
- [ ] **Choose the organization** — must have had significant change in the last 5 years
- [ ] Assign section owners (see below)

---

## Step 2 — Report Sections & Suggested Owners

| # | Section | What to Cover | Suggested Owner |
|---|---------|---------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Organization Selection** | Company background, nature of change, reasons for change, stakeholders affected | Member A |
| 2 | **Change Management Analysis** | Internal/external forces for change; type of change (strategy/structure/tech/people/culture); resistance to change; methods to reduce resistance; impact on employees & performance (with examples/evidence) | Member B |
| 3 | **HR Management Evaluation** | Recruitment & selection; training & development; performance management; compensation & benefits; retention initiatives — link each to how it supported the change | Member C |
| 4 | **Group Reflection & Value Appreciation** | Why change management matters; why employees should support change; which HR practices you personally value most; how this shapes you as a future manager/employee; lessons learned | All members (each contributes a reflection) |
| 5 | **Conclusion & Recommendations** | Key findings; recommendations for improving change management; recommendations for strengthening HR practices | Member D |
| — | **References (APA)** | ≥5 academic sources (journals, books, credible reports); proper in-text citations throughout | Whoever compiles — but everyone cites their own section |

---

## Step 3 — Assembly & Formatting
- [ ] Combine sections into one document
- [ ] Check formatting: TNR 12pt, 1.15 spacing, 1" margins, page numbers
- [ ] Confirm page count ≤ 16 pages (incl. tables/supporting material)
- [ ] Proofread for consistent tone across sections
- [ ] Attach marking rubric at the end
- [ ] Attach signed Group Assignment Declaration
- [ ] Rename file: `GROUP [tutorial code]_[group name]`

---

## Step 4 — Video Presentation (5–7 min)
- [ ] Script/slides covering: Intro → Change Analysis → HR Evaluation → Reflection → Conclusion
- [ ] Assign speaking parts (everyone should present)
- [ ] Record (Zoom/Teams/PowerPoint/Canva/iMovie)
- [ ] Check audio clarity and visual readability
- [ ] Rehearse once before final recording

---

## Step 5 — Submission
- [ ] Group leader uploads final PDF to Ebwise
- [ ] Double-check rubric + declaration are attached
- [ ] Submit before Week 6 deadline (25/9/2026)
